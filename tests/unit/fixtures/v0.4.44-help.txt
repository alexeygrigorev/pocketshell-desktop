Usage: pocketshell [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

  Unified server-side helper for the PocketShell Android client.

  Subcommands replace the separately-installed `quse`, `tmuxctl`, and `qr-
  share` CLIs. Today `usage`, `jobs`, `sessions`, `agent-log`, `repos`,
  `github`, `daemon`, and `qr-share` are wired up; more subcommands will land
  in follow-up rounds.

Options:
  -V, --version  Show the version and exit.
  -h, --help     Show this message and exit.
