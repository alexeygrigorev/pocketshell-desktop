Usage: pocketshell sessions [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

  Enumerate tmux sessions on the host.

  Thin wrapper around the existing `tmuxctl list` CLI: subcommands delegate to
  `tmuxctl` via subprocess and proxy stdout/stderr and exit codes verbatim.
  The output shape stays byte-identical to `tmuxctl list` so the Android-side
  `HostTmuxSessionListParser` keeps working when the app swaps its probe to
  `pocketshell sessions list` (issue #231).

  -h, --help  Show this message and exit.
